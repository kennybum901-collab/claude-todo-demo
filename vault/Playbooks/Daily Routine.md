# ☀️ Daily Routine (≈30 min)

1. Open [[Dashboard]] and check **🔥 Replies waiting for you**. Answer each one personally ([[Playbooks/Closing a Reply]]).
2. Check **🚨 Client sites down**. Paying clients come first.
3. Skim today's note in `Daily/` for bot errors.
4. Spot-check two or three new preview sites (open the `preview_url` in their lead notes).
5. Call phone-only leads: `python -m agency call-list`. Log the outcome under `## My notes`.
6. Weekly: review close rate in [[Dashboard]] and adjust niches or cities in `.env` (`LEAD_QUERIES`).
