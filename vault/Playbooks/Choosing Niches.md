# 🎯 Choosing Niches

Good niches: **high ticket per job, customers search online, many owners still have no website.**

| Niche | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Roofers, HVAC, plumbers, electricians | One job pays for the site many times over |
| Landscapers, pressure washing, cleaners | Lots of small operators with no website |
| Salons, barbers, nail studios | Easy upsell: online booking |
| Auto repair, detailing | Strong review counts to show off |

Track results per niche in [[Dashboard]] (the Dataview table groups by `niche`). Double down on niches with the best close rate, and drop the rest.
